Search text files with ripgrep. Hits and context lines come back as `lineNumber │ anchor│content` rows, editable with replace, insert, copy, or move without a new read; the `=== path ===` header and line numbers locate the match. Patterns are regex by default; set `literal: true` to search exact text, `ignoreCase` for case-insensitive search, and `context` for surrounding rows. Avoid backreferences, nested quantifiers, and quantified alternation. Searches respect `.gitignore`, always skip `.git`, and skip binary and image files silently. A match over 500 bytes is shown as a `...` fragment around the hit, but its anchor still covers the whole line. When cut by match limit, raise `limit` (default 100); when cut by rows/bytes, refine `pattern` / narrow path.
